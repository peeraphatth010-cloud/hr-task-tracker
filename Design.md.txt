## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an airy, hyper-modern executive workspace engineered specifically for HR task tracking and team orchestration. The aesthetic pairs the structural precision of Bento-style modular grids with soft, atmospheric dimensionality. 

Drawing from modern high-end product design, the interface pairs crisp, floating paper-white modules against an ice-tinted ambient canvas. Micro-interactions should feel tactile yet effortless: floating modules, crisp pill tags, dynamic counter widgets, and luminous accent callouts that turn high-density operational data into an inviting, glanceable dashboard. The tone is authoritative, optimistic, and radically uncluttered, eliminating cognitive fatigue during intensive workflow management.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by a soft atmospheric wash and punctuated with concentrated bursts of energy:

- **Canvas Background:** A subtle linear gradient spanning from `#F0F4F8` at the top left to `#E6EFFD` at the bottom right.
- **Card Surfaces:** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting cleanly above the ice-blue field.
- **Primary Accent (Electric Royal Blue):** A bold `#2563EB` to `#1D4ED8` gradient used for key actions, progress indicators, active navigation tabs, and pivotal interactive nodes.
- **Secondary Accent (Sun Yellow):** A warm `#FBBF24` (surface tint) shifting to `#F59E0B` (solid base), reserved for priority flags, approaching deadlines, status pings, and highlight callouts.
- **Neutrals & Typography:** Primary body and heading copy utilizes deep slate `#0F172A` for high-contrast legibility, with muted metadata rendered in `#64748B` and structural inner borders set to `#F1F5F9` or `#E2E8F0`.

## Typography

The typography leverages clean, geometric grotesque letterforms configured for seamless bilingual harmony between Latin characters and Thai scripts (paired with Noto Sans Thai / Prompt at the platform level). 

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered in tight, bold cuts (`-0.02em` tracking) to create a striking architectural presence across asymmetrical bento modules.
- **Body & Numerical Readouts:** Optimized for tabular scannability, task lists, and dense audit logs with standard spacing and generous line heights (`1.45` to `1.5`).
- **Caps & Micro-Labels:** Used on status badges, department flags, and metric category trackers with subtle positive tracking (`+0.02em`) to maintain sharp legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The interface is driven by a responsive Bento Grid model built on a 12-column layout for desktop environments:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Employs asymmetric column-spanning cards (`span-8`, `span-4`, `span-3`, `span-6`) with uniform `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) screen margins. Cards vary in vertical scale to accommodate varying data densities (e.g., active onboarding pipeline next to high-level sprint metrics).
- **Tablet (6 Columns):** Consolidates bento cards into balanced 3-column pairs or full-width 6-column modules, retaining a `1rem` (16px) gutter.
- **Mobile (Single Column):** Collapses into a vertical stack with `1rem` margin and `0.75rem` gap, prioritizing high-priority metric widgets and task checklists at the top.
- **Internal Card Padding:** Uses `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) for major visual containers and `1rem` (`space-md`) for compact telemetry tiles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on diffuse, multi-layered ambient drop shadows that simulate soft natural light falling over frosted planes:

- **Canvas Tier:** The gradient background (`#F0F4F8` to `#E6EFFD`) acts as the passive floor.
- **Resting Bento Card Tier:** Floating pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers lifted with a dual-stage shadow:
  - Ambient: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.07)`
  - Edge contact: `0 2px 6px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` for a crisp, glass-like rim highlight.
- **Hover & Interactive Focus:** Cards and actionable modules elevate slightly with a transform translateY of `-2px` and an enhanced shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** Crisp surface overlays with `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.14)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is defined by ultra-smooth, generous curvature that brings a friendly, polished aesthetic to workplace tooling:

- **Bento Modules & Major Cards:** Apply full `rounded-3xl` (24px to 32px depending on card scale) with squircular smoothing.
- **Interactive Controls & Fields:** Buttons, search fields, and form controls utilize pill silhouettes or continuous `rounded-xl` curves (12px to 16px).
- **Chips, Avatars, and Badges:** Fully circular or continuous full-radius pill styling (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Gradient fill from `#2563EB` to `#1D4ED8`, pure white text, fully rounded pill shape (`rounded-full`), padded `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle drop shadow: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Button:** Pure white background, `1px solid #E2E8F0`, dark text (`#0F172A`), shifts to `#F8FAFC` on hover.
- **Accent Action:** Gradient fill from `#FBBF24` to `#F59E0B`, deep slate text (`#0F172A`), for urgent priority updates or reward endorsements.

### Cards (Bento Boxes)
- Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces featuring `rounded-3xl` (24px desktop / 20px mobile) geometry.
- Content layouts incorporate asymmetric internal header bars, metric callout tiles, dynamic avatar overlaps, and task completion dials.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Pill-shaped tags (`rounded-full`) with semi-transparent tinted backgrounds. For "Urgent HR Escalation": background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`, text `#D97706`. For "Active Review": background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.10)`, text `#2563EB`.

### Checkboxes & Task Trackers
- Interactive checkboxes are `20px` squares with `rounded-md` (6px) corners. 
- Inactive state: `2px solid #CBD5E1`.
- Checked state: `#2563EB` fill displaying a sharp white micro-check with spring transition.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Floating pill-style containers (`rounded-full`) with pure white backgrounds, subtle inset shadows, and `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Focus state smoothly activates a `2px solid #2563EB` outline with an exterior glow ring (`rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`).

### Specialized HR Components
- **Progress Trackers:** Fluid pill track in `#EFF6FF` containing a rich `#2563EB` fill indicator with rounded ends.
- **Stacked Avatar Clusters:** Circular `36px` user profile elements with white `2px` borders overlapping by `-8px`, displaying active HR assignees.
